English

1. Scope

This Privacy Policy explains how Replay Recorder (the “App”), developed by yvhou, accesses, uses, stores, and shares information when you use the App.

2. Information the App accesses and why

  • Screen content: The App accesses the screen only after you approve Android’s system screen-capture prompt. This access is used to create standard recordings and user-requested replay clips.
  • Audio: Depending on your recording options and device support, the App may access playback audio, microphone audio, or both while capturing. Android audio permission is requested when needed; microphone audio is captured only in microphone or mixed-audio mode.
  • Notifications and overlay controls: Android requires a foreground-service notification while recording. If you enable floating controls, the App uses the “display over other apps” permission to show recording controls.
  • App settings and access status: Language, recording preferences, welcome-guide completion status, trial status, and Pro entitlement status are stored locally so the App can remember your choices and enable the correct features.
  • Purchase support diagnostics: When you explicitly restore purchases, a bounded summary is written to Android system logs: billing result codes, lifetime purchase count and state, verification outcomes, and only the final five digits of a recognized one-time order ID. It is not added to the App’s exported recording diagnostic report or automatically uploaded by the App. No full order IDs, purchase tokens, signatures, email addresses, or payment instruments are logged by this support code.
  • Local recording diagnostics: The App keeps a bounded local log of technical events such as recording configuration, thermal/memory/storage status, timestamps, and errors. These reports do not include recorded screen content, audio, typed content, payment details, or purchase tokens. They are not automatically uploaded by the App's recording code.
  • Google Play purchase information: To unlock or restore Pro, the App handles the purchased product, purchase state, purchase token, and signature supplied by Google Play. Tokens are used to confirm purchases with Google Play. Valid existing lifetime purchases remain supported. For subscriptions, RevenueCat processes an anonymous app user identifier, purchase tokens and product/order information, subscription status, and necessary app, device and network information, including IP addresses received by the service. The App does not provide a name, email, advertising identifier, screen content, audio or videos to RevenueCat. The App stores the verified subscription status, expiry, renewal/payment-issue state and bounded offline-verification timestamps locally. The developer uses the RevenueCat dashboard for purchase support, aggregate subscription reporting and subscription operations rather than a developer-maintained verification server.

3. Collection, transmission, and sharing

The App does not upload recordings or local recording diagnostics to a developer-operated server. It has no advertising or cross-app tracking functionality, and no app-account registration.

You can manage and share recordings through your device's gallery. You can also explicitly export a local diagnostic report and choose its recipient through Android's sharing interface. The destination app or service handles its copy under its own privacy policy. Gallery/cloud synchronization or backups that you enable independently are controlled by those services, not by this App.

After the current privacy disclosure is accepted, opening or returning to settings, restoring purchases, or starting a purchase can cause Google Play Billing to communicate with Google to retrieve prices, query ownership, and confirm purchases. Billing is independent of any age API and can communicate even if you do not buy Pro. After consent, the floating-control service also attempts entitlement verification every five minutes while it is running. Offline subscriptions remain usable for at most 72 hours within the verified paid period; a device restart requires online reverification. A server-verified active billing grace period has a bounded 24-hour refresh window. Failed requests do not clear a valid cached entitlement, but expiry/offline limits still apply. Valid lifetime purchases have no subscription expiry. Subscription expiry does not delete saved videos or prevent stopping and saving a recording already in progress. Google collects payment information directly under its terms; the App does not receive your full card number or other payment credentials.

The included Google Play Billing library also has its own diagnostic logging and transmission. It can send billing API results, connection/error information, billing-client session information, and technical device/app/network environment information to Google to operate and improve the billing service. This is separate from the App's local recording diagnostics and does not upload your recordings. Google's processing is governed by Google's Privacy Policy.

4. Storage, retention, and deletion

  • Recordings are saved to your device’s media library, normally under Movies/ReplayRecorder, and remain there until you delete them.
  • Replay keeps a bounded rolling window of encoded screen/audio data in memory. Older buffered content is released as the window advances. Recording and recovery can use temporary files in private app storage before a saved video is finalized.
  • Settings, welcome-guide completion status, trial/entitlement status, and local diagnostics remain in the App’s private storage until they are replaced, cleared through Android settings, or removed by uninstalling the App. Diagnostic storage is bounded, so older events may be overwritten.
  • The App excludes its private data from Android cloud backup and device transfer using its backup configuration. This does not delete recordings already saved in the shared media library or copies managed by other apps.
  • Clearing App data or uninstalling removes local settings and entitlement records; it does not cancel a Google Play purchase. A valid purchase can be restored using the same Google Play account. Uninstalling does not cancel an auto-renewing subscription; manage or cancel it in Google Play. Google and RevenueCat retain their own transaction and service records under their policies, and clearing App data does not delete those service-side records. Contact the developer at the support address below for requests about developer-controlled RevenueCat records. Requests are reviewed according to applicable retention, security and transaction requirements. RevenueCat uses its own service providers and may process data outside your country as described in its privacy policy.
  • Google's billing library manages its diagnostic queues and transmission separately from the App's bounded local recording log. The developer does not operate a cloud backup service for App data. Copies you export or share are controlled by you and your selected destination.

5. Security

The App uses Android’s permission system, system screen-capture consent, app-private storage, and local processing to reduce unnecessary data exposure. No method of storage is completely secure, so you should protect access to your device and review recordings before sharing them.

6. Children

The App is a general-purpose screen recording utility and is not designed specifically for children. It does not ask users to provide a birth date, identity document, age range or parental account to the developer. Google Play applies its own account, installation and transaction rules and may require verification or purchase approval. Parents can manage supported supervision and approvals through Google Play or Family Link. Do not email identity documents or a child’s age information to the developer. This description does not transfer every applicable developer obligation to Google Play.

7. Third-party services

The App relies on Android for device permissions and Google Play for supported distribution, purchases, restoration, and billing-service diagnostics. RevenueCat provides subscription verification and management; see RevenueCat’s Privacy Policy. Optional SDK diagnostic collection and automatic device-identifier collection are disabled in this integration. Necessary purchase/service processing still occurs. These services apply their own rules and privacy policies. Apps or services you select when sharing content are independent third parties.

8. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when App features, legal requirements, or data practices change. The current version will be published at this URL with a revised effective date.

9. Contact and data deletion requests

For privacy questions or requests, contact the developer at yvqiu18@gmail.com.

To request deletion of developer-controlled subscription data, email the address above with the subject “Replay data deletion”. Describe your request and provide enough purchase information to locate the record, such as the Google Play order reference; do not send passwords, card numbers, identity documents, recordings or purchase tokens. We may request limited information to verify ownership. Deletion is subject to applicable legal, security and transaction-retention requirements and does not cancel your subscription or delete Google’s records. Manage cancellation separately in Google Play.

10. Age information and upgrades

Starting with rc9 (version 16), the App no longer includes or calls the Google Play Age Signals API. It does not request age ranges, age-sharing status, range sources, installation identifiers or significant-change approval signals. Recording and purchase flows do not depend on this API.

Upgrading removes only the previous version’s local pending-access flag. Cleanup is retried if it cannot be completed and does not block recording or erase recordings, settings, trial records or Pro entitlement. The removed app age module no longer enforces its previous access decisions. Android and Google Play can independently apply their supported permissions and account, installation or transaction rules.

Purchase verification, pending transactions, cancellations, restoration and Google Play Billing diagnostics continue as described above. The developer does not operate an age-verification or parental-account service.
